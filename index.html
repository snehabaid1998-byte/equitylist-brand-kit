What's there today

What I'd clean up first

01 · Type

Eight typefaces, one brand

Geist is clearly the system. Inter, Poppins, Space Grotesk, Zalando Sans, Open Sans and Varela are drift from old pages and embeds.

Fix: Geist for words, Geist Mono for numbers. Nothing else.

02 · Color

Four near-identical violets

5F46FF 635BFF 6D4DFF 4F3AD9

All four are in use. Side by side, it's hard to say which one is the brand.

Fix: one brand violet, one pressed state, tints by token.

03 · Numbers

Two customer counts

The site says 650+ companies. The Wellfound listing says 550+. Small thing, but numbers are what EquityList sells.

Fix: one dated stats sheet that every asset pulls from.

13

The vesting chart

Rules I'd apply to every equity chart

1

Steps, not curves

Vesting is discrete. A smooth curve shows options that don't exist yet.

2

Labels on the data

No legends. People shouldn't have to match colors to a key.

3

Numbers in Geist Mono

Tabular figures, right-aligned, so columns line up.

4

A date on every number

"2,000 vested as of 9 Oct 2026", not "2,000 vested".

5

Axis starts at zero

A cropped axis makes a small grant look big. Never do it.

6

Value as a range

Potential value is a band with its 409A date and source, not one big number.

Sample: a hard concept, for an enterprise reader

How a trading window works

Open

Window closed: no trading by designated persons

48 hours

Open again

↑ Quarter ends

↑ Results announced

Who it covers

Designated persons, usually directors, senior staff and anyone with results data, plus their immediate relatives.

Even when open

Trades above the company's threshold need pre-clearance from the compliance officer first.

Where EquityList helps

Tracks window dates and routes pre-clearance requests, so ESOP exercises don't cause a violation.

Simplified from SEBI insider trading rules. Not legal advice.
